How Sentinel-2 Satellite Images Turn into a Risk Number

The Challenge: Glacial lakes sit at 5,000 meters altitude where rangers cannot visit daily.

The European Space Agency operates Sentinel-2 satellites. They photograph every mountain in Nepal every 5 days for free. But they don't just take normal photos — they capture Near-Infrared (NIR) light!

💧 Why Near-Infrared Detects Water (NDWI)

Water completely swallows Near-Infrared light, looking pitch black. Meanwhile, snow, ice, and mountain rocks reflect it brightly.

NDWI = (Green - NIR) / (Green + NIR)

If a pixel has NDWI > 0.30, that pixel is 100% water!

📐 From Pixels to the Risk Engine Number

1. Count water pixels: 12,800 pixels.
2. Each Sentinel pixel is 10m × 10m (100m²).
3. Lake Area = 12,800 × 100m² = 1.28 km².
4. Compare today's 1.42 km² vs last week's 1.28 km²:

Growth = ((1.42 - 1.28) / 1.28) × 100% = +10.9%

+10.9% is passed into lake_area_change in our Risk Engine!
